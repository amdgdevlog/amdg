Streamlining Compliance: Automated Subcontractor Onboarding with Salesforce Experience Cloud & AWS Lambda

General contractors can replace manual qualification bottlenecks with a fully automated, self-service onboarding engine by uniting Salesforce Experience Cloud with serverless AWS Lambda execution.

On commercial jobsites, general contractors are only as fast and reliable as their subcontractor network. Yet, onboarding new trade partners remains one of the most paperwork-intensive, high-risk processes in construction management.

Before a trade partner sets foot on site, project executives must collect, review, and verify dozens of mandatory documents—including Certificates of Insurance (COIs), OSHA safety records, tax forms, master service agreements, and trade licenses.

When this process relies on back-and-forth emails, physical binders, and manual spreadsheet tracking, critical compliance steps fall through the cracks. Assigning a subcontractor with expired General Liability coverage or an unverified safety rating exposes the entire project to immense legal liability and severe financial penalties.

The Problem: Administrative Drag & Unvetted Jobsite Risk

Risk management and pre-qualification teams spend hours chasing trade partners for updated documentation. Standard subcontractor onboarding workflows create three critical operational bottlenecks:

Three Critical Operational Bottlenecks:

  1. Slow Time-to-Jobsite: Manual document verification drags on for weeks, delaying contract execution and holding up trade mobilization on critical path schedule items.
  2. High Human Error in Document Review: Compliance managers manually reviewing hundreds of ACORD insurance certificates can easily miss subtle, critical flaws—such as incorrect additional insured endorsements, low policy limits, or upcoming expiration dates.
  3. Fragmented Communication & Lost Records: Subcontractors submit documents via disparate email threads to different project managers. Without a centralized portal, project teams lack real-time visibility into whether a sub on site is actually fully compliant.

The Solution: Automated Verification via Experience Cloud & AWS Serverless

By deploying a custom portal on Salesforce Experience Cloud backed by AWS Lambda and document intelligence services, contractors create a seamless, self-service onboarding portal that verifies compliance in real time.

How the Architecture Works

  1. Self-Service Subcontractor Portal: Trade partners receive an invitation to a branded Salesforce Experience Cloud portal. Subcontractors can update company profiles, input banking details for ACH payments, and drag-and-drop compliance documents directly into guided web forms.
  2. Serverless Document Processing via AWS Lambda: The moment a file (such as a Certificate of Insurance) is uploaded, an event-driven AWS Lambda function triggers automatically.
  3. Automated Verification Pipeline:
    • Extract Key Data: AWS Lambda calls AI extraction services to pull policy numbers, coverage limits, effective dates, and certificate holder details directly from the uploaded PDF.
    • API Validation: AWS Lambda executes background API calls to state licensing boards and insurance verification databases to validate that licenses and policies are active and legitimate.
  4. Instant Salesforce Status Sync: The Lambda function compares extracted coverage metrics against project-specific requirements set in Salesforce.
    • Full Compliance: The record in Salesforce is instantly marked as Qualified, auto-generating contract templates and clearing the subcontractor for site access.
    • Non-Compliance: If coverage limits fall short or a policy expires within 30 days, the portal automatically flags the specific deficiency and notifies the subcontractor to upload a corrected document.

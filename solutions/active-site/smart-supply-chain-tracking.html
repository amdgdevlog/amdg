Real-Time Material Visibility: Smart Supply Chain Tracking

Pairing passive/active RFID infrastructure and AWS IoT Core with Salesforce Order Management to gain real-time, automated control over material movement from factory floor to final installation.

In commercial construction, jobsite progress relies heavily on just-in-time material delivery. Structural steel columns, custom curtain wall glass, precast concrete panels, and high-value MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing) assemblies must arrive on site in exact sequence.

However, once raw materials leave the fabrication plant or supplier warehouse, general contractors usually lose visibility into their status. Project managers rely on phone calls, static shipping manifests, and manual gate logs to track critical materials.

When a flatbed carrying custom structural steel gets delayed in transit—or arrives at the wrong gate without the field team knowing—installation crews stand idle, mobile cranes sit unused at $500 an hour, and downstream trades face scheduling conflicts.

To eliminate jobsite blind spots and keep projects on schedule, forward-thinking contractors are digitizing their building material supply chains. By pairing AWS IoT Core and passive/active RFID infrastructure with Salesforce Order Management, construction firms gain real-time, automated control over material movement from factory floor to final installation.

The Problem: Black-Box Logistics, Site Bottlenecks, & Schedule Slippage

Managing material delivery across multiple vendors, logistics carriers, and laydown yards introduces severe operational risks:

Three Primary Operational Risks:

  1. Lack of Transit Visibility: Project managers are routinely surprised by delivery delays, learning that essential structural components are missing only after installation crews are already on site.
  2. Congested Laydown Yards & Lost Materials: When materials arrive on large jobsites without automated scanning, components are often misplaced in sprawling laydown yards. Teams waste hours searching for specific tagged members, sometimes ordering duplicate replacements unnecessarily.
  3. Manual Order Reconciliation: Project engineers spend hundreds of hours manually cross-referencing paper delivery tickets against purchase orders and project milestones in their ERP or CRM, delaying vendor invoice approvals and progress payments.

The Solution: End-to-End Material Tracking via AWS IoT & Salesforce Order Management

By placing industrial RFID tags or IoT cellular trackers on material shipments and deploying automated RFID gate readers at site entrances, contractors turn physical material movements into live data triggers inside Salesforce Order Management.

How the Architecture Works

  1. Tagging & Dispatch: Suppliers or fabricators attach low-cost, durable RFID tags or BLE beacons to structural components at the factory. Item identifiers are linked directly to specific purchase orders inside Salesforce.
  2. In-Transit Tracking via AWS IoT Core: As delivery trucks move toward the jobsite, IoT gate scanners, cellular gateways, or Amazon Sidewalk-enabled tracking nodes read tag data and stream location updates to AWS IoT Core. Amazon Location Service monitors geofences around the jobsite, automatically firing alerts when a shipment is 30 minutes away.
  3. Automated Site Gate Ingestion: When the delivery truck passes through the jobsite entry gate, fixed RFID reader arches automatically scan the entire load in seconds without requiring the driver to step out or manually scan barcodes.
  4. Salesforce Order Management Sync: An AWS Lambda function transforms the raw RFID scan events and updates the corresponding record in Salesforce Order Management.
    • The purchase order status automatically updates from In Transit to Delivered / On-Site.
    • Specific component line items are logged with exact laydown yard coordinates.
  5. Intelligent Field Scheduling: Salesforce automatically notifies the site superintendent and rigging crew that the materials have arrived, dispatches crane capacity for offloading, and updates linked tasks in Salesforce Field Service.

Business Impact & Results

Digitizing the construction material pipeline turns reactive site logistics into a predictable, automated workflow:

Zero

Idle Crew Time

Rigging crews, crane operators, and trade contractors are dispatched only when materials are physically confirmed on site or within the final approach geofence.

95%

Reduction in Gate Processing Time

Automated RFID gate scanning processes entire truckloads in seconds, eliminating delivery truck queues and blocking adjacent traffic.

100%

Elimination of Lost Material Rework

Real-time laydown yard mapping ensures every beam, panel, and spool is indexed by location in Salesforce, preventing costly re-orders of "lost" site inventory.

Instant

Automated Invoice Matching

Material receipt data in Salesforce Order Management automatically reconciles vendor delivery tickets against purchase orders, accelerating payment cycles and eliminating billing disputes.

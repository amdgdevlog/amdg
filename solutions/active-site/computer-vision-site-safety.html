Proactive Safety Enforcement: Computer Vision Site Monitoring

Pair real-time video analytics from AWS Vision AI infrastructure with automated field workflows in Salesforce to instantly detect unsafe behaviors and enforce site compliance.

Construction remains one of the most hazardous industries worldwide. According to OSHA, unsafe site conditions, personal protective equipment (PPE) non-compliance, and heavy equipment proximity lead to thousands of preventable site injuries and costly project shutdowns each year.

Traditionally, Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) compliance relies on manual site walks by safety officers. While dedicated, human inspectors cannot monitor every square foot of a multi-acre commercial jobsite simultaneously. Minor safety oversights—such as a worker removing a hardhat in an active hoist zone or an unauthorized vehicle entering a trench area—often go unnoticed until an incident occurs.

To move from reactive incident reporting to real-time risk prevention, general contractors are turning to automated computer vision edge intelligence. By pairing real-time video analytics from AWS Vision AI Infrastructure with automated field workflows in Salesforce Service Cloud and Salesforce Field Service, construction firms can automatically detect unsafe behaviors and enforce site compliance instantly.

The Problem: Blind Spots, Paperwork Delays, & Regulatory Exposure

Manual safety management leaves contractors exposed to significant operational and legal risks:

  1. Intermittent Inspection Coverage: A safety manager conducting a walk-through on one side of a site has zero visibility into compliance gaps on the far end of the development.
  2. Lagging Incident Logging: When near-misses or safety violations are spotted manually, they are often recorded on clipboards or physical forms. It can take hours—or days—for safety logs to reach the corporate EHS director in Salesforce.
  3. Escalating Regulatory Fines & Insurance Costs: Unaddressed safety violations lead to severe OSHA penalties, work-stop orders, and skyrocketing Workers' Compensation insurance premiums that eat away at project margins.

The Solution: Real-Time Edge Analytics via AWS & Salesforce Field Workflows

By linking on-site IP network cameras to AWS Edge Vision AI Services (utilizing AWS IoT Greengrass and Amazon Rekognition Custom Labels) and routing alerts into Salesforce, safety teams achieve continuous 24/7 visual compliance enforcement.

How the Architecture Works

  1. Edge-Based Video Stream Processing: On-site IP network cameras stream live video to localized edge computing devices running AWS IoT Greengrass. Machine learning models run locally at the edge, analyzing frames in real time with low latency.
  2. Automated Safety Detection: The custom-trained computer vision model continuously scans for high-risk hazards:
    • PPE Compliance: Missing hardhats, high-visibility vests, protective eyewear, or safety harnesses at elevated heights.
    • Restricted Zone Violations: Personnel entering prohibited zones around active cranes, high-voltage equipment, or open excavation pits.
    • Equipment Proximity Alerts: Ground workers standing within dangerous blind spots of reversing bulldozers or excavators.
  3. Multi-Tiered Alerting Pipeline:
    • Instant Site Alert: Upon detecting a high-severity violation (e.g., entering a fall-hazard zone without a harness), the edge system instantly triggers a local audible alarm or site strobe light.
    • Salesforce Safety Incident Logging: A low-bandwidth event payload, including a snapshot thumbnail, is pushed via AWS Lambda into Salesforce Service Cloud. The system creates a native Safety Incident record tagged with the exact time, zone, and sub-tier trade involved.
  4. Automated Corrective Actions: Salesforce executes automated business rules based on the violation severity:
    • Sends an immediate push notification to the Site Superintendent's Salesforce Mobile App.
    • Logs recurring safety violations against the subcontractor’s master vendor record, automatically assigning mandatory safety refresher modules in Salesforce Experience Cloud.
